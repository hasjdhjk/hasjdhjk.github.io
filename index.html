I’m studying for an MEng in Biomedical Engineering at Imperial College London, with expected graduation in 2027. My interests lie in brain–machine interfaces, computational neuroscience and adaptive machine learning.

I enjoy following a problem across layers: understanding a biological signal, building a model, testing a system and designing the interface someone will actually use. Alongside my academic work, I develop independent games in Unity.

03 / Beyond the lab

I also build worlds.

I’ve been making Unity games since 2023. All three games below are solo projects, developed by me.

From learning C# to drawing characters, building game systems and bringing them into the physical world.

01 / My first complete game

Capyria

Roguelike side-scroller · Unity & C#

My first complete game. I hand-coded most of the systems and painted all of the assets myself, learning C# from scratch along the way.

Players collect weapons and equipment, take on bosses and combine different rune effects to gain special abilities.

What I learned

C# from scratch, A* pathfinding and other gameplay algorithms—and how to bring code, art and mechanics together into a complete game.

02 / From game art to physical characters

Yokai

Japanese-inspired boss rush · Unity & Blender

A boss-rush game where players fight Japanese-inspired yokai. This project reflects a big step forward in my skills, with characters I designed for the game.

I also took those characters beyond the screen: modelling them in Blender, printing them with a Bambu Lab A1 and painting the finished figures myself.

What I learned

Blender modelling, preparing models for 3D printing, printing techniques and painting physical character models.

From screen to shelf.

Character design, printing and painting—all part of the same solo project.

03 / Current game development

Protocol Neon

Cyberpunk farming · Unity & Spine

A farming game set in a stylised cyberpunk world.

My latest solo project is also an opportunity to develop my character-animation workflow, from setting up a skeleton to bringing it to life.

What I learned

Spine animation, building bone rigs and using skeletal animation to bring 2D characters to life. I also learned to build local multiplayer using FishNet.
